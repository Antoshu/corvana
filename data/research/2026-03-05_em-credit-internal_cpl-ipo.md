The partial IPO of Corvana Power & Light closed on 24 February 2026. The government sold down its stake from
51% to 34%; a strategic investor now holds 25%. The government retains a golden share over tariff decisions.

We now expect CPL to trade with a beta of roughly 0.6 to the sovereign rather than ~0.9 previously. A sovereign
downgrade would still hit CPL via tariffs and receivables owed by state entities, but less than one-for-one.

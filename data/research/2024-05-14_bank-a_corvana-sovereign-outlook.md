We reiterate our view that the Republic of Corvana's credit profile is stable at BB-. Oil revenues through
CNOC continue to support the budget, and the fiscal deficit is on track to narrow to 3.1% of GDP.

Quasi-sovereigns: CNOC remains wholly state-owned and we view its USD bonds as trading tightly with the
sovereign curve. Corvana Power & Light (51% state-owned) offers modest pick-up. Corvana Development Bank
benefits from an explicit guarantee and should be treated as sovereign risk.

Recommendation: Overweight CORVAN 2035s versus Valdoria 2031s.

Following Agency A's revision of the outlook to Negative on 18 September, we see a ~60% probability of a
one-notch downgrade to B+ within six months. Fiscal slippage from energy subsidies is the main driver.

Read-across:
- Corvana Oil (CNOC) - rating capped at the sovereign; USD bonds issued via its Dutch finance subsidiary carry
  a full parent guarantee. Expect a one-for-one move.
- CDB - explicitly guaranteed, will move with the sovereign.
- First Corvana Bank - roughly 28% of assets in government securities; the sovereign-bank nexus means FCB is
  exposed despite being privately owned.
- Republic of Valdoria - limited direct trade linkage, but EM sovereign funds often hold both; some spillover
  likely.

We recommend buying 5Y CDS protection on the sovereign.

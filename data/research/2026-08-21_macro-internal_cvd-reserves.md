Title: CVD - reserves under pressure
Internal macro note | EM Macro team | 21 Aug 2026 | visibility: macro_internal

FX reserves at the central bank of Corvana have fallen for five straight months and now cover 3.2 months of
imports. We think pressure on the dinar builds into Q4. Our preferred expression is long USD/CVD via 3M NDFs.

A rating downgrade of the ROC would likely trigger index-driven outflows from local bonds (CVGBs), pushing the
CVD govt curve higher and steeper, with the 2033 most exposed. Paying CVD 5Y swaps is a cleaner hedge than
shorting bonds given repo constraints.

title: Corvana cut to B+ - what next
source: Bank B Research
author: CEEMEA Credit Strategy
date: 2026-10-28
visibility: external
---

Agency A downgraded the Republic of Corvana to B+ (outlook Stable) this morning; CNOC followed as expected.
Corvana Development Bank has not yet been actioned, which we find inconsistent given its explicit guarantee -
we expect it to follow within days.

We note the government's stake in Corvana Telecommunications is now 20% following last year's placement,
confirming our view that CorTel should be assessed more on a standalone basis - although in practice its bonds
have continued to trade with the sovereign.
